# Skill: mentor-motivation

## Purpose

Skill gaps are fixed with teaching; **motivation gaps are fixed with design**.
This skill turns "I can't keep going" into a concrete, tiny, doable plan —
and gives the learner a visible record of wins and streaks to fight doubt
with evidence.

---

## When to Use

Use this skill when:

- Sessions/check-ins were skipped ("I didn't do anything this week").
- The learner expresses burnout, frustration, or imposter doubt.
- A challenge or exercise has been abandoned or stalled.
- The check-in shows a repeating pattern of missed goals.

---

## Step-by-Step Workflow

1. **Blocker interview** (never assume laziness):
   - Time? Energy? Confusion? Fear? Priority conflict?
   - Ask: "What actually stopped you — what was the moment you didn't start?"
2. **Design the smallest next step**:
   - Shrink the task until it takes ≤10 minutes ("open the file and write one
     test"). Momentum > size.
   - Re-scope the roadmap if the plan doesn't fit the real time budget
     (`mentor-roadmap`): halve milestones, keep wins achievable.
3. **Habit design**:
   - Same time/place trigger ("after dinner, terminal opens").
   - **Streak protection rule**: never miss two days in a row — one tiny
     step still counts.
4. **Wins log** (`mentor/wins.md` via `learning-log`):
   - Append every achievement: completed exercises/challenges, level-ups,
     positive feedback, brave questions, stuck-moments conquered.
   - When the learner doubts themselves, **quote the log**: "You said you
     can't do X — on <date> you did <related win>."
5. **Streak dashboard** (`mentor/streak.md`):
   - Table: week | sessions | hours | ratings | wins | streak days.
   - Updated at each session end and check-in; the check-in shows a
     one-line dashboard ("4 sessions, 6h, streak 12 days").
6. **Follow up** — at the next session, check the small step first; celebrate
   the smallest completion. Never guilt — re-plan instead.

---

## Outputs

- `docs/learning/mentor/wins.md` — the confidence log (append)
- `docs/learning/mentor/streak.md` — consistency dashboard (overwrite)
- Roadmap re-scope via `mentor-roadmap`
- Diary entries reflecting the recovery plan

---

## Notes

- Motivation is cyclic: plan for dips instead of being surprised by them.
- If blockers are structural (job, health), re-scope the rhythm to casual
  (`mentor-adapt`) — protecting the relationship beats protecting the plan.
- Wins must be evidence-linked (dates, files) or they don't fight doubt.
